# HeartOut — Master Brand Identity & Guidelines Board

![HeartOut Master Brand Kit Overview](/frontend/public/assets/brand/heartout_brand_kit.jpg)

> *"Where every quiet story finds a home."*

---

## 🏛️ 1. Brand Strategy & Core DNA

* **Category**: Empathetic Storytelling & Personal Digital Sanctuary.
* **Core Metaphor**: *"The Open Journal & Unfolding Heart"* — The geometric heart mark opens like the crisp leaves of a hardcover journal, symbolizing vulnerability, unexpressed feelings, and therapeutic release.
* **Tone of Voice**: Grounded, sincere, quiet, empathetic, literary, and human.

## 🎨 3. Spatial Rules & Interaction Philosophy

1. **Content Over Chrome**: No decorative gradients or noisy badges may compete with the written word.
2. **Concentric Double-Bezel Geometry**: Cards utilize outer shell frames (`rounded-[2rem] p-1.5`) and inner machined cores (`rounded-[calc(2rem-0.375rem)]`).
3. **Restrained Emotional Accentuation**: Saturated color is strictly reserved for emotional reaction states and crisis safety interventions.
